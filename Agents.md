## ✨ The idea

Make it easier to **plan spending**, *notice patterns*, and save toward goals without making money management feel intimidating.

### A simple monthly rhythm

1. **Plan** — set a spending limit for each category.
2. **Track** — add income and expenses as they happen.
3. **Review** — compare your plan with your actual spending.
4. **Adjust** — make next month’s plan work better for you.

### Helpful building blocks

- 📊 A quick monthly overview
- 🧾 Expense categories and transaction history
- 🎯 Savings goals with progress tracking
- 🔔 Optional reminders for bills and budget check-ins
- 🔒 Privacy-minded handling of financial information

## 💡 A useful rule of thumb

> A budget is a plan, not a punishment. Leave room for real life, and revise it when your circumstances change.

## 🧮 Quick calculation

```text
remaining = monthly_income - total_expenses - planned_savings
```

For example, if income is `$3,000`, expenses are `$2,250`, and savings are `$400`, then **$350 remains** for other priorities.
